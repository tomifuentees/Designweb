# Fuentes de la investigación

Investigación hecha el 19 de noviembre de 2026 (zona America/Argentina/Salta).

## Formato y herramientas

- Design Tokens Community Group — [Format Module 2025.10](https://www.designtokens.org/tr/2025.10/format/), Final Community Group Report, 28 de octubre de 2025. [Color Module](https://www.designtokens.org/tr/2025.10/color/). Anuncio de la versión estable: [28 de octubre de 2025](https://www.w3.org/community/design-tokens/2025/10/28/design-tokens-specification-reaches-first-stable-version/). Schema: `https://www.designtokens.org/schemas/2025.10/format.json`. El informe **no** es una recomendación W3C; es el formato estable del Community Group.
- Tailwind CSS — [v4.0](https://tailwindcss.com/blog/tailwindcss-v4) (22 de enero de 2025), [Theme variables](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/theme), [Adding custom styles](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/adding-custom-styles), [Functions and directives](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/functions-and-directives). Discusión de `@theme static`: [tailwindlabs/tailwindcss #16436](https://github.com/tailwindlabs/tailwindcss/discussions/16436).
- Utopia — [Type calculator](https://utopia.fyi/type/calculator/), [Fluid typography is easy](https://utopia.fyi/blog/utopian-typography-is-easy/), [Clamp calculator](https://utopia.fyi/clamp/calculator/).
- thanor.ai — [Design tokens for AI-built websites, in the W3C format](https://thanor.ai/blog/design-tokens-for-ai-websites).
- Motif / ASV Labs — [AI Frontends Look Alike? Start With Design Tokens](https://motif.asv-labs.com/answers/design-tokens-for-ai-built-frontends/).
- r/vibecoding — [All AI websites look the same…](https://www.reddit.com/r/vibecoding/comments/1rr0dwy/all_ai_websites_and_designs_look_the_same_has/) (11 de marzo de 2026); comentario del 16 de abril de 2026 sobre `CLAUDE.md` con tokens.

## Tells que estas escalas cortan

- Utsubo — [The "Built with AI" Tell](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/built-with-ai-trust-signals-2026), 23 de junio de 2026 (motion without purpose, identical radius/shadow).
